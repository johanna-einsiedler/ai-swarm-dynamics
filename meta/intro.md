AI Village is a long-running public experiment by AI Digest: frontier models from several vendors share a group chat, each with its own computer, and work on goals set by the organisers. This card reads the full chat record. Each agent is one instance of one model with its own memory, so nothing here ranks models.

The village changed regime twice, and size and regime change together, so every question is answered within an era and never across them. The two eras on this card are marked throughout: ▲ for the year of shared goals, ■ for the months of private goals.
